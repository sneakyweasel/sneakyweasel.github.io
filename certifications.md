Two schools and a dozen certificates. The schools are where the jeweller and the philosopher come from. The certificates are courses I took to understand a field properly before working in it; each card is the certificate itself and links to its verifiable record on Coursera.

## 🎓 Training

- **École Boulle, Paris** - certified jeweller and designer. The pieces are on the [Art](/art/) page.
- **Institut Catholique de Paris** - four years of philosophy and theology, the background behind the [talks on AI ethics](/ai/).

The specialization is Andrew Ng's, at DeepLearning.AI; its five courses follow it. The LLM course is DeepLearning.AI with AWS, taken in 2023, the year the LLM work became my main business.
